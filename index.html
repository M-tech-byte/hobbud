Keep your HobBud safe 🛟

Create a backup file that you can keep on your laptop. It can include your profile, hobbies, session history, mood journal, weekly plans, and gallery photos.

⬇️ Save a backup copy

Download one backup file and keep it somewhere safe, such as Documents or a USB drive. You can make a fresh backup whenever you add important things.

Your backup is a file on your device. It is not automatically sent to the cloud.

⬆️ Restore a backup

If you ever lose your saved browser data, choose a backup file to restore it. Restoring replaces the HobBud data currently in this browser with the backup's data.

Only select a backup file that you created from HobBud. Keep copies of your original photos too.

💚 Three simple safety habits

1. Download a backup after making lots of changes.
2. Keep a copy of the backup and your original photos in a safe folder.
3. Don't clear browser data until you've made and checked a backup.
